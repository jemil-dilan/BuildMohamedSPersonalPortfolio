# Surface: portfolio home

<!-- impeccable:surface-brief 1 -->

## Scope and mode

Single-page portfolio at `/`. Persuade: a hiring manager or client decides within two minutes whether to start a conversation. Weighted to hiring, open to contract work.

## Audience and job

Backend hiring managers and recruiters (primary), clients (secondary), local peers. They arrive from a CV or LinkedIn and need: who this is, what he has shipped, whether the claims are real, and how to make contact. They scan on a mid-range Android on mobile data as often as on desktop.

## Action

Primary: open a project, then email. Secondary: LinkedIn or GitHub. The page must make the two concurrent roles and the four projects legible without reading every paragraph.

## Proof and content

Four projects (ExpoPrepa, Lifekora, Suku, Movia) in the fixed order; three roles with the Lao Sarl / Logiaspace overlap marked concurrent; four internal talk topics; two education entries; the verified stack inventory in two tiers; contact details. No screenshots, photography or CV file exist — do not fabricate them.

## Constraints

The full accuracy contract in PRODUCT.md binds every string. Bilingual FR/EN, French default, toggle visible at every breakpoint without flag icons. WCAG AA, visible focus, reduced motion, 375px verified in French. No invented metrics, durations-in-words, or forbidden technologies.

## Direction

Décollage: a pasted hoarding torn back by hand, where depth is history. The current work is the top sheet; older layers stay visible under the tear and are never erased to look clean. Navigation is a row of pasted tabs; project cards are sheets with a torn corner the visitor lifts to read the role. Memorable moment: the first viewport's red poster tears away at its lower edge to reveal the chalk band carrying the two concurrent roles, and lifting a project's corner keeps it open as a record.

## Unresolved

No CV PDF. The contact section must not ship a dead download; use "email me for the CV" until a file exists. Project links: ExpoPrepa and Lifekora have live URLs; Suku has no public repo, so no code link. Movia stays unlinked while its prototype shows invented figures.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A portfolio as a pasted wall. The current work is the top sheet, every older layer is still there under the tear, and nothing is erased to look clean. It refuses the centred hero over a row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: paste-grey board #C9C2B2, bleached poster chalk #EDE7D8, poster red #C63A2A and poster blue #2C4C6B as full fields, halftone dots and fibrous white tear lips. Archivo Black for display, Archivo Narrow for condensed facts, Courier Prime for labels and figures, Public Sans for body. Every control is a pasted tab, a stamped label, or a lifted flap.

STORY: The visitor reads a name and a claim, sees two roles in parallel on the exposed band, lifts four project corners to read the exact role and stack, understands he is early but already trusted, and emails or opens a project.

FIRST VIEWPORT: full-bleed red poster; MOHAMED / NFONKA at ~150px across two lines top-left; a black thesis bar beneath; a torn bottom edge at 63% of the height revealing a chalk band with an outlined NOV 2024 and the two-role fact; a pasted CTA label over the tear at right; FR/EN tabs top-right; nav as pasted tabs along the top edge.

FORM: torn hoarding (catalog challenger, chosen over the assigned ledger); seed:2fc5d65a349b6f7c.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
